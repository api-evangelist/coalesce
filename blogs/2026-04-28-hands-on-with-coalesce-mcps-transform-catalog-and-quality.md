MCPs are incredibly useful, but taking them from experimental to something that’s used across teams requires a more deliberate approach. At Coalesce, with the acquisition of SYNQ, the toolset now spans the full data operating layer. Transform for building data models, Catalog for governance and discovery, and Quality for data observability.
